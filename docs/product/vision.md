# Product Vision

**Last updated**: 2026-09-22

## Vision

Help households plan meals and shop together with minimal friction, from any phone, using a private self-hosted app they control.

## Product principles

- **Household-first simplicity**: fast weekly planning, minimal setup burden.
- **Mobile-first by default**: core journeys optimized for phone use.
- **Real-time collaboration**: shared grocery state updates instantly.
- **Privacy and ownership**: self-hosted core, user-controlled data.
- **Practical reliability**: backup and recovery are first-class concerns.

## Target outcome

By default, two people can split shopping across stores and maintain one synchronized grocery list with low coordination overhead.
